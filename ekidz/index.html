Frequently Asked Question

Everything you want to know about Narayana eKidz – the happiest & most trusted nursery

Q1. What makes Narayana one of the best nursery schools?

The reasons why we claim so are that Narayana’s Nursery school, also known as eKidz, provides a nurturing, kind, and happy environment for children. Kids here learn through play, stories, and love builds strong foundations. This helps them create joyful early learning experiences.

Q2. Can we trust Narayana as parents for a pre-school?

Yes! Parents trust Narayana with their children because of our emphasis on both care and education. Our qualified caregivers teach children to grow up feeling confident and curious, while helping to ensure that they develop into socially strong individuals.

Q3.How can I know if Narayana is a play school that is right for my child?

Upon entering our facility, you will immediately sense the warmth and sincerity of our staff as you walk through our classrooms, which create the feeling of a second home. You will know that your child will feel safe, respected, and excited to come to school each day.

Q4. Why is Narayana a great preschool near me?

Narayana nurtures the overall development of children. At Narayana, your child learns about letters, numbers, and other life experiences while learning how to share, voice their feelings, and express their emotions.

Q5. Is Narayana similar to a Montessori pre-school near me?

Yes. Our learning approach is child-friendly and activity-based. Children explore, touch, ask questions, and learn at their own pace, just like the Montessori method.

Q6. How does Narayana prepare children in their KG schools

Do you want your child to have a solid foundation? Children must create one when they are young. Their language development, growth of the mind, and self-confidence help children make the transition from Kindergarten to the higher classes.

Q7. Are teachers caring and trained at Narayana’s eKidz?

Indeed, our teachers have sufficient experience to understand the thoughts of young students. To make every child feel appreciated and supported, they teach with love, patience, and attention.

Q8. To what extent can parents become involved in their child’s experience in the nursery school?

As partners in the learning experience, parents are ensured regular opportunities to discuss with the teacher of their child. Regular updates are ensured to provide parents with information about their child’s progress and well-being.

Q9. What is the typical daily experience in Narayana eKidz for a child?

Every morning in Narayana eKidz starts with smiling and welcoming children. They paint, sing, play, listen to stories, and learn via enjoyable activities. Every moment is pleasant, happy, and safe.

Q10. How can Narayana eKidz help a shy kid?

Every child is unique. We acknowledge that. Our teachers never speak harshly. Rather, they speak softly and offer encouragement gradually. They make the quiet ones smile, talk, and mingle.

Q11. Is Narayana eKidz a safe & hygienic place for kids?

Yes, safety is our first promise. Our classrooms are clean, bright, and attractive to children. The children are constantly supervised, giving their parents peace whenever they leave them.

Q12. In what way does Narayana eKidz contribute to the formation of good habits in children at a young age?

At home, we also teach children good habits such as washing their hands, sharing their toys, taking turns, and speaking politely. These habits impart good values for life.
